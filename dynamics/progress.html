        How these numbers are produced

        Every block of work moves through the same pipeline before it counts here: a full pass over the block, an independent dense audit, an adjudication step where audits disagree, and a correction sweep whose output is folded into the governing ledger. Q5 documents are annotated by two independent lanes and sealed only by a reconciliation of both. This page reads its data live from the committed snapshot in the microcosm-dynamics repository.
