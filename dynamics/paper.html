Microcosm Dynamics: an open, scored dynamic microsimulation model

The paper describes the model as it stands: the transitions it fits on the Panel Study of Income Dynamics, how its gated components are scored under terms locked before each scored run and changed only by public amendment, four registered blind tests against DYNASIM3's published results, and what remains to build.

population
Starts from Microcosm's certified CPS frame (166,302 people in 57,240 households, 2024), or from PSID cohorts for the DynaSim tests.
gates
Earnings, family, disability and population transport each pass a registered gate; claiming, disability-insurance entitlement and entrants run without one.
blind tests
Four one-shot tests against DYNASIM3: a COLA cut lands within 0.74 points in every age group; three locate specific differences.
next
Forward earnings in the projections, the retirement earnings test, a claiming model, and benefit rules executed in Axiom.
