strategy 06 · dynamics

Time.

Microcosm's certified layer is cross-sectional: one snapshot, calibrated to one year of administrative targets. Retirement and social-insurance policy analysis needs a person aged forward through earnings, family structure, disability, and mortality — the benchmark U.S. models that do this (DYNASIM, MINT, CBOLT) are closed: internal to government, tied to restricted administrative records, or reachable only through institutional relationships. Dynamics is an open alternative, built as an extension of the certified cross-sectional layer rather than a replacement for it.

01 — the 30-second version

Where the model stands.

Microcosm Dynamics fits the transitions of individual lives on the Panel Study of Income Dynamics and runs them forward year by year. Each gated component is scored on held-out or external data under terms locked before its scored run, and every scored run is committed whether it passes or fails. Any later change to a gate's terms is a public amendment; four of the passes rest on amendments adopted after an earlier candidate failed. Four registered blind tests compare the model with DYNASIM3's published results. The projection tests do not yet draw forward earnings, apply the retirement earnings test or model claiming responses; that is the next stage of work.

01built · gated

Gated components

Generated earnings histories, the forward earnings law, family formation, household composition, disability and the transport onto Microcosm's CPS frame each pass a registered gate. Claiming, disability-insurance entitlement and entrants run without one.

02reported

Four blind tests against DYNASIM3

Each is registered in public, with one scored run compared after its output is committed. A one-point COLA cut lands within 0.74 points of DYNASIM3 in every age group; the other three locate specific differences between the two models.

03design

Claims bounded by horizon

The design scores near-term components against administrative publications and publishes long-horizon outputs as sensitivity surfaces over documented assumptions, not as a forecast. Gate 3, which would score the near-term components, has not yet run.

04planned

Next: forward earnings

Carrying the forward earnings law into the projections, applying the retirement earnings test and modelling claiming, each admitted only through its own registered gate.

02 — scope

One validation domain first.

U.S. Social Security is the first validation domain: a single program whose administrative statistics can score near-term components, and whose published DYNASIM3 results the model can be compared with.
