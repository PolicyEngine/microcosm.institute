01 · composition

Assemble the survey multispine first

  1. Harmonize ACS and CPS ASEC. Preserve source identity and entity links. Record renames, unit conversions, derived fields and assumptions as explicit operations.
  2. Assemble the combined survey frame. Allocate survey mass across the two spines before adding clone or donor channels.
  3. Apply the PUF clone to that combined frame. Tax-detail enrichment is an operator on the ACS + ASEC population; it does not define a separate ASEC–PUF base.
  4. Complete values and assign supported geography. Keep observed values distinct from donor draws and sampled geographic cells.
  5. Evaluate, calibrate and verify. Check the full candidate and each pruned export against their declared national and district analysis scope.

This is the candidate's execution order, not a claim that every stage has completed. A graph should expose each transformation's inputs, outputs, parameters, source evidence and validation results. A drawn dependency does not imply successful execution.

02 · verified component

County and district are one joint draw

The actual national crosswalk contains 2,462 PUMAs and 87,841 joint PUMA/tract/congressional-district cells across all 50 states and DC. Its populated-block join represents 331,449,281 people from the 2020 Census and covers 436 congressional districts including DC. These are source-geography counts, not estimates from a calibrated Microcosm population. The evidence summary records the artifact identity and source hashes.

Each household receives one population-weighted joint cell within its PUMA. County comes from the selected tract. Drawing county and district independently could create a pair with no populated-block support, even when each separately overlaps that PUMA.

An ACS record keeps its observed 2020 PUMA. An ASEC record keeps its assigned state and draws a PUMA within that state. The seed and geography vintage are explicit. Showing the sampled tract does not change the county or district draw; it does not turn that tract into an observed residence.

The crosswalk uses 2020 PUMAs, counties and tracts with 119th Congress districts. All 53 source files matched fresh, TLS-verified responses from the exact Census URLs. A further 53,462 invented-household checks passed joint support, PUMA/state preservation and tract-output invariance. Source records include the tract-to-PUMA relationship, CD119 block equivalency file and the state PL 94-171 archives listed in the JSON summary.

County remains a model input and tract output is optional. This component does not certify county or tract analysis, nor establish calibration quality for the final household file.

03 · remaining verification

Keep each verdict separate

Source identity and joint geography: the national crosswalk passed. That establishes supported assignments, not correct household weights or complete model coverage.

Enrichment controls: the 65-target enrichment mechanism passed 57 invented-data controls covering 56 person outputs and nine tax-unit outputs. Both initial execution and required replay passed, including after reloading the stored frame. Fable's independent source review agreed with the correction and closed all seven findings; it did not rerun execution or recompute file hashes. Integration with the genuine combined survey candidate remains pending. These are mechanism checks, not validation of the final population; the evidence summary records their scope.

PUF source ingestion: the source check retained 207,692 ordinary records and excluded four disclosure records. It verified ingestion and typed serialization; it did not run enrichment models or growth to the target year. This source check is a prerequisite to applying the PUF clone to the combined survey frame.

Donor integration: initial SCF source preparation stopped on a signed-wage assumption that is under investigation. A refused source is not counted as a completed donor stage.

Release verification: the assembled candidate still needs model lookup coverage, donor and entity checks, weighted geography checks including NYC, national and congressional-district calibration, and export verification. The calibration dashboard must identify and inspect the exact final file, including the effects of pruning.

Calibration can change how much weight supported records receive. It cannot create missing geographic combinations or repair an unsupported assignment. The graph therefore retains separate source, support, model-coverage, calibration and export results.

This candidate work informs the composition, support and calibration research questions. Component checks are not new benchmark findings in those papers.
