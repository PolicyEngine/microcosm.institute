01 · composition

Assemble the survey multispine first

  1. Harmonize ACS and CPS ASEC. Preserve source identity and entity links. Record renames, unit conversions, derived fields and assumptions as explicit operations.
  2. Assemble the combined survey frame. Allocate survey mass across the two spines before adding clone or donor channels.
  3. Assign one Census block per household. Preserve observed survey geography constraints, then derive larger geographies from versioned mappings. The ten-node survey prefix now implements this order; native source coverage remains under verification.
  4. Apply the PUF clone and complete values. Enrichment clones inherit the household's location. Tax-detail enrichment operates on the combined ACS + ASEC population. Observed values and modeled draws retain separate provenance.
  5. Evaluate, calibrate and verify. Check the full candidate and each pruned export against their declared national and district analysis scope.

The current ten-node survey prefix implements block-first assignment before cloning. Its complete thirteen-node age-calibration extension passes cold execution and required replay on invented original sources. The earlier joint tract/district operator remains a separate historical path. Each transformation records inputs, outputs, parameters, source evidence and validation results; native population acceptance is still pending.

02 · geography

One small area determines the household's geography

The actual national crosswalk contains 2,462 PUMAs and 87,841 joint PUMA/tract/congressional-district cells across all 50 states and DC. Its populated-block join represents 331,449,281 people from the 2020 Census and covers 436 congressional districts including DC. These are source-geography counts, not estimates from a calibrated Microcosm population. The evidence summary records the artifact identity and source hashes.

The existing operator selects one population-weighted joint cell within a PUMA and derives county from its tract. The revised design assigns a Census block first and derives larger geographies from that anchor. The prior joint-cell checks establish source support; they do not certify the new block assignment.

Shared country-neutral import, atomic assignment, derivation and integrity-gate operators now pass 34 invented-data controls, including execution and reuse through the actual graph runtime. Country declarations select constraints, stable household identities, sampling stages and mapping conventions. The US block adapter additionally passes 13 invented controls for block retention, leading zeroes, mapping consistency and stable assignment after pruning. An actual invented graph also assigns geography before cloning and verifies inherited mappings through required replay. Current observed geography now passes 11 source-qualification controls and 14 graph-node controls, including literal household draw keys, unknown locations and receipt-mutation refusals. The complete ten-node US prefix adds five passing controls, and its independently reconstructed calibration budget adds eight. One full thirteen-node age-calibration control passes both cold execution and required replay. A compatibility control preserves the earlier predictor and PUF-host interface. Native block/OA support and release integration remain pending.

Native block-source preparation: thirty-six invented byte-source controls and thirty-five population-only Census API controls pass. The native path will request only P1_001N block populations and independent state totals, then join CD119 and tract-to-PUMA mappings. Delaware is the first source check; national survey assignment requires all fifty states and DC. These controls do not certify a downloaded source or geographic fit.

ACS observed PUMA and ASEC source geography constrain the assignment. The household identity, seed, source support and boundary vintages must determine a stable location that enrichment and pruning retain. Inferred block detail remains modeled.

The crosswalk uses 2020 PUMAs, counties and tracts with 119th Congress districts. All 53 source files matched fresh, TLS-verified responses from the exact Census URLs. A further 53,462 invented-household checks passed joint support, PUMA/state preservation and tract-output invariance. Source records include the tract-to-PUMA relationship, CD119 block equivalency file and the state PL 94-171 archives listed in the JSON summary.

The UK follows the same design with a nation-specific anchor: 2021 Output Area in England and Wales, 2022 Output Area in Scotland, and 2021 Data Zone in Northern Ireland. Its existing area ladder supplies larger geographies from one selected row. Stable household-keyed sampling and explicit graph derivations still need integration. Constituency and ward mappings can use best-fit conventions, which must retain their source and vintage. See NISRA geography, Scotland's census report and ONS methodology.

Geographic consistency is a build invariant even when a model does not consume block or tract. It does not, by itself, certify county or tract analysis or establish calibration quality.
