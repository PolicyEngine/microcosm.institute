01 · composition

Assemble the survey multispine first

  1. Harmonize ACS and CPS ASEC. Preserve source identity and entity links. Record renames, unit conversions, derived fields and assumptions as explicit operations.
  2. Complete the multispine, including clones. Allocate survey mass and construct the initial support and PUF clone channels before geographic assignment.
  3. Assign one Census block per resulting household. Distinct clones may receive different blocks, subject to observed survey geography constraints. Use stable source and clone identity, then derive larger geographies from versioned mappings.
  4. Complete enrichment while retaining location. Tax-detail enrichment operates on the combined ACS + ASEC population. Observed values and modeled draws retain separate provenance; calibration and analysis views do not redraw geography.
  5. Evaluate, calibrate and verify. Check the full candidate and each pruned export against their declared national and district analysis scope.
