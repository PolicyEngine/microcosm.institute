Constructing representative microdata with sub-national weights

The two-stage pipeline behind the certified Microcosm U.S. release: fill the file with weighted, regime-gated, chained quantile-regression-forest imputation; represent the population with target-informed L0 selection and calibration against 32,633 administrative targets. Ghenis, Juaristi, Woodruff & Ogorek.

Read the paper (PDF) Paper & experiment code Live calibration dashboard
stage one
Weighting where the design is informative is first-order: the identical estimator fit unweighted is 6× worse on net-worth fit, its 99th percentile 1.9–2.3× the holdout’s.
stage two
Gates select 57,240 of 337,704 candidate records; refit publication weights reach a 4.74% loss against 5.07% dense and 7.55% random.
deployed
The support ships as the certified Microcosm U.S. release and scores the One Big Beautiful Bill Act at national, state, and district level.
